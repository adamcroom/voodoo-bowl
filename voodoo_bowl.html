Football like you've never played before...

  • Hit any key to begin.
  • Move your player using the following keys: 'I'=UP, 'J'=LEFT, 'K'=DOWN, 'L'=RIGHT.
  • Avoid zombies and hit referees, which are worth 2 points.
  • You have 4 downs and you need to advance 10 yards to make 1st down.
  • Touchdown is worth 7 points.
  • More zombies after each touchdown.
  • Score as many points as you can in 60 seconds.
